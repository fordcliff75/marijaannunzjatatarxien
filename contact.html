    How your submission is handled

    Your name, email address, message and selected photographs are sent privately through FormSubmit to the website owner’s email inbox. Your email is used to reply; it is not displayed on the website.

    FormSubmit says it retains message submissions for 30 days and does not retain uploaded files in its submission archive. Delivered emails and attachments remain in the owner’s private mailbox until deleted. The website does not store your form entries in your browser.

    Photographs and stories are considered for publication only when you select the separate permission above. Nothing is published automatically, and your contact details are not published with your contribution.

    You may use this form to ask about your submission or request its deletion. Spam and invalid submissions may be rejected.
