Tarxien Social Club Victoria

A community connected to Tarxien’s festa traditions and Our Lady of the Annunciation.

Historical background

According to the Maltese Community Council of Victoria’s club history, the club was formed in 1976 and its first Annunciation feast celebration took place in Queenscliff that year.

Source: MCCV association history. Historical details are separate from current announcements; check the club’s Facebook Page for updates.
